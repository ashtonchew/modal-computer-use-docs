## How to read the evidence

Current results includes eligible measurements. Latency evidence also records historical, experimental, candidate, estimated, rejected, and unverified claims. Each dated report preserves the full method and links its sanitized artifact.

[Reproducibility](/benchmarks/reproducibility) defines the checks for source revisions, configurations, failures, cleanup, and publication.
