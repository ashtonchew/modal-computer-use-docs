## Measurement boundaries

- Computer Step measures an immediate frame after ordered action dispatch.
- First visual change remains experimental.
- Application readiness and task success remain application-owned.
- Cold allocation and startup are reported separately from warm trajectory work.
- Historical provider comparisons describe complete recorded paths with different configurations.

## Evidence rules

- Pin source revisions with full commit SHAs.
- Keep configuration, timer boundaries, warmup, failures, retries, replacements, and cleanup in the artifact.
- Preserve rejected and historical evidence.
- Keep raw output private and publish sanitized artifacts.
- Run live Modal measurements only with an approved cost ceiling and cleanup plan.
