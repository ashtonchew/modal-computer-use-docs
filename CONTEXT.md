# Benchmark results

This context defines the measurement types used in Modal Computer Use performance documentation.

## Language

**Warm-operation benchmark**:
A benchmark that measures one operation after the desktop and client connection are ready. Desktop creation and cleanup are outside its timer.
_Avoid_: Article campaign, warm-operation campaign

**Modal product benchmark**:
A benchmark that measures an implementation choice or capacity within Modal Computer Use.
_Avoid_: Product experiment, product evidence

**Complete action-to-frame benchmark**:
A benchmark that measures ordered action dispatch through the next decoded and validated screenshot.
_Avoid_: Fused-turn estimate, action-frame campaign
